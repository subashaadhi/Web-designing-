The Art of Jewelry Making: From Sketch to Finished Product

At METALDOM, we merge masterful craftsmanship with innovative technical concepts to transform your creative ideas into stunning fine jewelry. Here is how we bring your vision to life:

1. Design & Sketching

Every single masterpiece begins with an idea. Our designers work closely with you to perfectly illustrate every detail of your concept, whether through hand-drawn sketches or digital rendering.

2. Wax Carving & 3D Modeling

Traditional hand-carved wax molds or advanced 3D modeling technologies breathe structural life into your designs. This step ensures geometric precision and allows for seamless adjustments prior to mass manufacturing.

3. Wax Injection & 3D Printing

Once approved, designs are transformed into production wax patterns. Traditional methods involve injecting wax into rubber molds, while modern techniques utilize high-resolution 3D printing to create exceptionally precise resin or wax models.

4. Casting

The wax pattern is safely encased in a heat-resistant investment mold and replaced with your metal of choice (Gold, Silver, Brass, etc.) via the lost-wax casting method. The final result is a raw jewelry casting awaiting meticulous finishing.

5. Hand Finishing & Polishing

Master artisans hand-finish each piece: polishing raw surfaces to a brilliant mirror-like shine, executing secure gemstone settings, and adding custom textures or engravings. This critical step guarantees a truly luxurious standard.

6. Custom Brand Packaging

Your jewelry is carefully presented in bespoke, custom-branded packaging—engineered to elevate the premium unboxing experience and powerfully project your brand identity.

From concept to finished piece, METALDOM crafts more than just jewelry—we build a legacy. Ready to bring your designs to life?
